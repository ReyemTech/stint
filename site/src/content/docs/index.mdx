<section class="section section-alt">
  <h2 class="section-title">Built around how you already work.</h2>

  <div class="feature-grid">
    <div class="feature">
      <div class="feature-icon">⌘</div>
      <h3 class="feature-title">Keyboard-first GUI</h3>
      <p>Menu bar popover for a fast start-stop loop. ⌘1 / ⌘2 / ⌘3 to switch routes. Esc to dismiss. No dock icon clutter.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="feature">
      <div class="feature-icon">⎘</div>
      <h3 class="feature-title">CLI in your terminal</h3>
      <p>The same Rust binary that lives inside the .app also ships as a standalone CLI. <code>stint start "thing"</code> from anywhere you can type.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="feature">
      <div class="feature-icon">↻</div>
      <h3 class="feature-title">Local-first sync</h3>
      <p>Every mutation persists to SQLite immediately. A background worker drains the queue to Solidtime with exponential backoff. Offline-safe by construction.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="feature">
      <div class="feature-icon">📅</div>
      <h3 class="feature-title">Calendar one-click logging</h3>
      <p>Connect a Google Calendar. Click a past meeting to log it as a time entry with title, project, and time range pre-filled.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="section">
  <h2 class="section-title">What stint <em>doesn't</em> do.</h2>
  <p class="section-lede">
    The shape of a product is also defined by what's left out.
  </p>

  <ul class="anti-list">
    <li>
      <span class="anti-mark">⛔</span>
      <div>
        <strong>No account.</strong>
        <p>Solidtime is the account. stint is a client.</p>
      </div>
    </li>
    <li>
      <span class="anti-mark">⛔</span>
      <div>
        <strong>No telemetry.</strong>
        <p>Outbound traffic goes to your Solidtime instance, your calendar provider (if connected), and an update manifest on <code>github.com</code> once every 24h. No analytics. No crash reports. No "anonymous usage data."</p>
      </div>
    </li>
    <li>
      <span class="anti-mark">⛔</span>
      <div>
        <strong>No paywall.</strong>
        <p>MIT-licensed. Free forever. Fork it and ship your own version if you want — that's the point of MIT.</p>
      </div>
    </li>
    <li>
      <span class="anti-mark">⛔</span>
      <div>
        <strong>No browser tab.</strong>
        <p>If you want to type into a web app, use Solidtime's web app. stint is for the moments between web apps.</p>
      </div>
    </li>
  </ul>
</section>
